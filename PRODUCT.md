# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Church members signing in to access their account, groups, giving, events, and saved information.

## Product Purpose

Provide church members with a clear, welcoming, and trustworthy way to enter their member account. Success means members can complete sign-in confidently and understand how to recover access when needed.

## Operating Context

Members may sign in from desktop or mobile devices before engaging with church activities and account services.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Deliver a polished responsive user-interface flow without connecting an authentication backend.
- Include form validation, password visibility controls, a remember-me option, account recovery, and success feedback.
- Use placeholder church identity and copy that can be replaced when real brand assets become available.
- Preserve the existing Next.js application stack.

## Evidence on Hand

No real church name, logo, brand assets, authentication service, testimonials, or other proof assets have been provided. Future work must not present placeholder content as factual.

## Product Principles

- Make account access feel welcoming rather than institutional.
- Keep the primary sign-in path simple and immediately understandable.
- Communicate validation, recovery, and success states clearly.
- Support members comfortably across desktop and mobile devices.
- Keep placeholder identity easy to replace with the church's real brand.
